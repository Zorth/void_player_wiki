#location [[Zenith]]

> [!abstract]
> A subterranean tomb located north of [[Sharan]].

The Tomb of the Black Sun is a sunken temple marked by an obelisk peak. It contains multiple traps, sarcophagi, and a mysterious portal to a shadow/fog domain (likely the [[Foglands]]). The vault within was sought by the [[Obsidian Eye]]. It was previously guarded by a shadow dog beast and a Door Guardian construct.
