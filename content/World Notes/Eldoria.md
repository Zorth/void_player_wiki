The Kingdom of Eldoria is a forest realm ruled by [[Alaric Silverleaf|King Alaric Silverleaf]]. They are specialists in medicine and herbal healing. The population lives in tree-top cities and forest villages.

The capital is [[Silvergate]], built directly into the forest.

## Culture and History

During the Dwarven Crisis, Eldoria gave sanctuary to fleeing dwarves. The military consists of rangers and druids, but their primary reputation is for healing magic.

The elves act as caretakers of the land. They are generally peaceful but defensive of their traditions and borders.
