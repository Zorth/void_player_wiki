#location [[Zenith]]

The Kingdom of Muris features dense forests, rolling hills, and stone castles. It exports timber and stone and is known for its heavy construction and fortresses.

Muris relies on mercenary guilds for defense and civil services. In exchange, these guilds are granted castles and land to manage.

The capital is [[Crownspire]], ruled by [[Saxon Parisi|King Saxon Parisi]]. 

## Elite Knights
Noble families provide the kingdom's elite knights. They are highly disciplined martial experts and serve as the primary line of defense.
