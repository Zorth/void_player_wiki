#organization [[Hilonor]]

One of the five ruling families in the [[Evolution Epicentre]].

> [!quote]
> Trade within the region of the Zakna family is slowly coming to a halt due to roadside ambushers. Making entire shipments disappear alongside the merchants themselves.

## Notable Locations
- [[Zaknahir]]: The capital of their region.

## Current Events
Recent roadside ambushers have disrupted trade to the point where merchants prefer traveling in larger groups for safety. [[Tassarion Joven]] has sought adventurers to accompany his cargo to [[Zaknahir]].
