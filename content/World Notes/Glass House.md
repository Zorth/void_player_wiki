#location [[The Void]]

> [!Quote]
> The Void needs their farms, of course. And that's what the glass house is. A large greenhouse filled with plants and magical lights and irrigation paths for all your planting needs. Whether it be for magical potions and concoctions or just regular food.
>
> Make sure to register correctly when using the glass house. Whilst anyone can visit, harvesting without permission is stealing and planting without a permit is considered a crime against The Void.
