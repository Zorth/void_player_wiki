#organization [[Kalogeron]]

The **Divine Council** is the high ruling body of [[Kalogeron]]. Its members are the leaders of the city's most influential Great Houses, and they are said to embody the vision and power of the [[Old Pantheon]].

The Council has a partnership with adventurers of [[The Void]], rewarding those who assist in handling brewing trouble within the city.

## Council Members

- **[[Charis Lu'an Is]]** (House [[Lu'an Is]]) — Embodying the [[Old Pantheon#Deities|Dawnflower]].
- **[[Syriss Canción]]** (House [[Canción]]) — Embodying the [[Old Pantheon#Deities|Eternal Song]].
- **[[Iro Sou Prabuddh]]** (House [[Prabuddh]]) — Embodying the [[Old Pantheon#Deities|Master of Masters]].
- **[[Adoras Adalet]]** (House [[Adalet]]) — Embodying the [[Old Pantheon#Deities|Gilded Fist]].
