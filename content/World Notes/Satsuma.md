The Satsuma Shogunate is ruled by [[Takashi Masamune|Shogun Takashi Masamune]]. They are known for swordsmanship and a strong navy. The land has citrus groves, coastal trade hubs, and mountain villages.

The Shogunate is very sceptical of magic and openly anti-magic.

The capital is [[Sunshiro]], located near [[Jade Peak]]. 

## Culture and Values
The people value loyalty and martial skill. The architecture is fortified but follows traditional styles.

## Mercenary Conduct
Agents of [[The Void]] must follow these rules in the Shogunate:
- **Peace-Binding**: Weapons must be peace-bound in [[Jade Peak]] and noble districts.
- **Crimes**: Theft, unauthorized magic, and disturbing customs are punished by banishment or execution.
- **Sanctioned Action**: Work must be sanctioned by a noble house.
- **Hide Magic**: They understand we use magic when hiring us, but refrain from using magic, especially on them, when possible.

Helping one house will draw scrutiny from their rivals.
