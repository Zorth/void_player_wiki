# Journeyman Exam

The **Journeyman Exam** is a specialized rank quest that an [[pc|Apprentice]] must complete to be promoted within the [[The Void#The Guild of The Void|Guild of the Void]].

## Overview

Upon reaching **Level 7**, a character becomes eligible to undertake their Journeyman Exam. This is not a standard mission but a curated trial intended to prove the character's growth and reliability as a member of the Guild.

- **Requirement**: Character Level 7.
- **Appointment**: One of the [[_gming|Voidmaster]]s will be appointed to design and run the quest specifically for the candidate.

## Promotion

Successful completion of the exam grants the rank of **[[_ranks#Journeyman|Journeyman]]**, unlocking several [[_ranks#Journeyman Benefits|benefits]], including:
- Sponsored quests through the Guild.
- Private residence in **The Lots**.

> [!info] 
> For more details on the rank system, see the [[_ranks|Ranks of The Void]] guide.
