Khal is a settlement in the [[Evolution Epicentre]], located north-east of [[Gurgich]].

> [!quote]
> The academy of Khal teaches the basics for those that ever want to join one of the large families, recently they have been having trouble acquiring test subjects.

The academy recently requested help with a growing goblin population that has made procuring "course material" (test subjects) difficult for their usual employees.
