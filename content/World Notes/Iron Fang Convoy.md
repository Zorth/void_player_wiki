#organization [[Hilonor]]

An organization spread throughout Hilonor that functions as an adventurer's guild, collecting info on quest opportunities and assembling parties for unfulfilled tasks.

> [!quote]
> Each person that picks up the adventuring life will almost certainly join the convoy. Receiving certain privileges outside of just the iron fang convoy depending on their rank.

## Ranking System

Members are required to visibly wear their rank, usually in the form of a keychain with a number of [[Silver Fangs]].

- **1 to 5 Fangs**: Rank based on combat proficiency.
- **Canine** / **Molar**: Higher titles for those independent or part of an organization.
- **Incisor**: The highest title, held by only 8 people, described as "human apocalypses."

The leadership (those who decide rankings) is located on the [[New Continent]].
