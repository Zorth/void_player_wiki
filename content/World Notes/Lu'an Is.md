#organization [[Kalogeron]]

House Lu'an Is is one of the member houses of the [[Divine Council]]. Its matron, [[Charis Lu'an Is]], is known for her compassion and kindness, reflecting the spirit of the [[Old Pantheon#Deities|Dawnflower]].

### Beliefs & Practice
- **Open Practice**: House Lu'an Is is the only house in [[Kalogeron]] that openly practices its religion, following the ways of the Redeemer, the [[Dawnflower]].
- **Redemption**: They believe anyone can be redeemed, a philosophy that clashes with the martial beliefs of House [[Krieg]].

### Notable Connections
- Friendly with House [[Thihna]], both being clerical houses.
