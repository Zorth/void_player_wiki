# Master Exam

The **Master Exam** (also known as the **Guildmaster Rank Quest**) is the final promotion trial within the [[The Void#The Guild of The Void|Guild of the Void]].

## Overview

Characters who have achieved the rank of [[_ranks#Journeyman|Journeyman]] and reached **Level 14** are eligible for the Master Exam. This trial is significantly more difficult than the Journeyman Exam and often involves a complex mission that impacts the character's chosen region.

- **Requirement**: Character Level 14 and Journeyman rank.
- **Process**: Completion of a Master-level quest designed by a [[_gming|Voidmaster]].

## Promotion

Successful completion grants the rank of **[[_ranks#Guildmaster|Guildmaster]]**, entitling the character to substantial [[_ranks#Guildmaster Benefits|benefits]], including:
- Becoming Guildmaster of a specific region.
- Responsibility for quests and missions within that region.
- Direct influence on the **Void Council**.

> [!info] 
> For more details on the rank system, see the [[_ranks|Ranks of The Void]] guide.
