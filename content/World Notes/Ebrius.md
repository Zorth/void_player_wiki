#organization [[Kalogeron]]

> [!abstract]
> The house of trade in [[Kalogeron]].

**House Ebrius** is led by [[Lazar Ebrius]]. The house dominates the city's commerce, managing and owning the **Market District** of [[Kalogeron]]. Lazar Ebrius is married to [[Lady Fortuna]], the leader of House [[Fortuna]].
