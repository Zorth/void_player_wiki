#organization [[Kalogeron]]

> [!abstract]
> A powerful house in [[Kalogeron]] that runs the [[Valor District]].

**House Andreía** is led by Lady Andreía (full name unknown). The house is responsible for overseeing the **Valor District**. It is known for having the largest number of vassal houses in the city and maintains close ties with House [[Krieg]], as Lady Andreía serves as the Under-General to [[Lord Krieg]].
