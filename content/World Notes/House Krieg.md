#organization [[Kalogeron]]

> [!abstract]
> A house focused on militia and security in [[Kalogeron]].

House Krieg is led by [[Lord Krieg]]. It is responsible for militia and security matters. The house works closely with [[Lady Andreía]], who serves as the house's Under-General. Their martial beliefs sometimes clash with the redemption-focused philosophy of House [[Lu'an Is]].
