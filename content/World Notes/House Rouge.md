#organization [[Kalogeron]]

> [!abstract]
> The house that runs the [[Red Casino]] in [[Kalogeron]].

House Rouge is led by [[Kalista Rouge]]. They manage the [[Red Casino]] and work closely with House [[Agrati]] to maintain order in the Harbour District. Kalista is supported by a right-hand-maiden who handles bookkeeping.
