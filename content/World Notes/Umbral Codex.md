#location [[The Void]]

> [!Quote]
> The Library of The Void is a small building on the outside, with arches and ornate windows decorating it. The inside is much bigger however with magical candles lighting it for easy reading. With rows and rows of books lining the walls and corridors of shelves about many universes.
>
> Books that get lost often tend to appear in the library, with "The Bookrift" contained in a glass chamber often popping in random books from worlds. The librarians work hard sorting these treasures of knowledge.
