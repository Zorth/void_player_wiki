#organization [[Kalogeron]]

> [!abstract]
> The house that manages the Harbour District of [[Kalogeron]].

House Agrati is led by [[Ioz'om Agrati]]. They are responsible for the management of the [[Harbour District]] and work closely with House [[Rouge]] to maintain order there. Ioz'om Agrati is known for having a formidable temperament.
