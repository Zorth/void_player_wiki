#npc [[Kalogeron]]

> The leader of House [[Wonders]].

**Alphons Wonders** is the head of House [[Wonders]]. He serves as a diplomat for House [[Esoteric]] and its vassals. In addition to his diplomatic duties, he is known to dabble in the creation and study of magical scrolls.
