#npc [[Zenith]]

Sultan of the [[Sassanid|Sassanid Empire]]. A charismatic and scholarly leader often accompanied by his legendary sphinx companion. He rules from his tower in [[Sharan]].

He has one of the [[Coins of Zenith]] and uses its power for evil and total control.
