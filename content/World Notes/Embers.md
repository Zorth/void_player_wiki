#organization [[Zenith]]

The Embers are fanatics devoted to flame and destruction. They believe the world must be destroyed by fire to be reborn.

## Origins & Purpose
- Use sacrificial fire magic.
- Organized into cells across the kingdoms.
- Led by "Keepers of the Flame."
- Seeking artifacts and knowledge for an unknown plan.

## Operations
- **Satsuma Shogunate**: Operating undercover.
- **Muris**: Acting more openly.
- **Interests**: Looking into dwarven history (specifically their extinction) and elven secrets.
- **Recent Events**: Burned a village to hide their tracks.
