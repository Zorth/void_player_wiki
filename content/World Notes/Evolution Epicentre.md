#location [[Hilonor]]

The Evolution Epicentre is a country on the [[Old Continent]] of [[Hilonor]].

> [!quote]
> One of the Countries in the land of Hilonor, studying the improvement of the body. Done trough a variety of ways, mainly transplants of limbs or organs of different or even the same race as the patient, from time-to-time parts of monsters are used.

## Structure

The country is divided among five different families, with a large central region serving as common ground for collaborative efforts and shared findings.

> [!quote]
> The Country is divided, split up due to a great dispute the subject of which is lost due to time... There are 5 different families that rule over the Evolution Epicentre, the largest region being a common ground where their findings are shared and collaborative efforts happen.
