#world [[Kalogeron]]

> [!abstract]
> A deity known as the Redeemer.

The **Dawnflower**, also known as the Redeemer, is a deity whose religion is openly practiced within [[Kalogeron]] primarily by House [[Lu'an Is]]. The followers of the Dawnflower believe in redemption and kindness for all.
