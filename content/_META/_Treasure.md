Keep in mind most of this treasure will be in (magical) items and thus will lose half of its value when sold. You should be able to expect a 50/50 split of items the session's level and the level + 1.

## Quick Loot Distribution

To easily and fairly divide loot, the "buy back" method is often used by players of the void.

1. Sell all items at half item price.[^1]
2. Split the total coinage.
3. "Buy back" the items you want for half item price.

Example:
> For a party of 4 the loot is:
> - 8 sp (or 0.8 gp)
> - 1x [Hide Shield](https://2e.aonprd.com/Shields.aspx?ID=11) (worth 2 gp)
> - 1x [Palstave](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=506) (worth 4 sp, or 0.4 gp)
>
> With the items sold for half price it's:
> $0.8 + (2 / 2 = 1) + (0.4 / 2 = 0.2) = 2 gp$
>
> Divided by 4, each adventurer gets 0.5 gp or 5 sp.
>
> Add this to your inventory, then any someone can buy back the Hide shield for 1 gp or the palstave for 2 sp.
>
> Keep in mind that there's only 1 item of each so multiple adventurers can't "buy back" the Hide Shield or Palstave.

[^1]: Art, Materials, and Components are sold for full price instead of half.
