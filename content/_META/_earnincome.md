Earning Income is one of the most common downtime activities as it allows you to earn a little extra cash.

1. Pick a skill you are at least **Trained** in.
2. Explain to your Voidmaster how you are using this skill to earn money between your last session and this one.
3. Tell the Voidmaster your character's level, they will tell you the earned amount based on your character (task) level and success level.

**Critical Success** You do outstanding work. Gain the amount of currency listed for the task level + 1 and your proficiency rank.  
**Success** You do competent work. Gain the amount of currency listed for the task level and your proficiency rank.  
**Failure** You do shoddy work and get paid the bare minimum for your time. Gain the amount of currency listed in the failure column for the task level.
**Critical Failure** You earn nothing for your work and are fired immediately. You can't continue at the task. Your reputation suffers, potentially making it difficult for you to find rewarding jobs in that community in the future.
