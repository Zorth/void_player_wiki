# Unholy Labyrinth (Floor 1)

> [!abstract]
> [[Renkar]], having heard of a labyrinth north of [[Wamerwick]], called the [[Unholy Labyrinth]] wished to explore the ruins.
> After gathering a crew they set out to [[Wamerwick]], there they hired a taxi service to dive them near the labyrinth. After an altercation with some plant-like creatures, they successfully managed to clear the first floor before finding a doorway leading downwards to lower levels. Here the party decided to turn back and head home.
