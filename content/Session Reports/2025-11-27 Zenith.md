# Trouble on Hakim's Farm

> [!note]
> The current state of this article is written by Jasper as a parsing of the notes of Twanny as posted on discord. I was not in this session and thus they may not be accurate.

> [!abstract]
> The party was hired by [[Madam Mor]] to investigate livestock attacks on [[Hakim]]'s farm in the [[Sassanid|Sassanid Empire]]. They tracked a large creature to an oasis where they were ambushed by two teleporting, poison-fog-generating lynxes. After a difficult battle, they defeated the creatures, destroyed the magical runes causing the fog, and returned to the farm with a specimen. They learned the creatures might originate from the [[Foglands]].

## Notes

- Hired by Madam Mor to investigate livestock attacks on Hakim's farm in a desert world.
- Traveled via the Well of Worlds to the city of [[Sharan]], warned by Will of local unrest.
- At the farm, found dead camels with signs of acidic decay and tracks of a large creature.
- Followed the tracks to an abandoned caravan site, where they were questioned by the Aether Wing force about magical anomalies.
- Found a communication rune left by the Aether Wing captain that read "Return to me."

> [!question]
> What is this rune? Is it from the aether wings? They also spotted a [[Sandworm Riders|sandworm rider]], is it from them?

- Tracked the creature to an oasis and were ambushed by two teleporting, poison-fog-generating lynxes.
- Discovered the fog was generated by magical runes on a large rock outcropping.
- After a difficult battle, the party destroyed the runes, dispelled the fog, and defeated the lynxes.
- Returned to Hakim, who identified the creatures as mutated predators possibly from the "Unexplored Lands" beyond the Shogunate's border wall.

> [!question]
> Creatures escaping the [[Foglands]]? Worth exploring and containing.

- Confirmed Madam Mor's arrangement for a lizard egg and left a lynx corpse with Will for study.
