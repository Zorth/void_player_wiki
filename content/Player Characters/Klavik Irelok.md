#pc/apprentice 
Klavik is a weathered but willing artisan locksmith, fascinated by intricate locking mechanisms and the art of smaller mechanical or magical designs. It took him a while to realize that crafting massive orcish weaponry and armor just wasn’t his calling. 

Originating from [[Sythian]] - [[Boverland]], Klavik got recruited to the [[Veiled Claw]], a thieves guild operating in the region. A youngster used for his obsessions to help out with minor crimes, he got deeper and deeper within the organization. 

Eventually grown up and having grown a proper conscience, Klavik's ideals started to clash with the leader of the Guild, and Klavik wanted out.
The only problem was they wouldn't let him go like that and the Guild had the leverage to keep him tied down. But all that changed when the perfect opportunity arrived with Klavik being recruited into [[The Void]].

Finally out, Klavik has put his skills for the better. A better life, but whenever back in Sythian, he can't help to look over his shoulder once in a while because of the traces of his past.
