The [Learn a Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2366) downtime activity can be taken in the void by any class that has a repertoire of spells, such as a spellbook for wizards. Any common spell can be chosen, uncommon or rare spell scrolls must be found or otherwise obtained.

To learn the spell you must use the corresponding skill of the tradition of the repertoire you are adding the spell to. For example, if you were a [cleric](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=5) with the [bard multiclass](https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx?ID=3) archetype, you couldn't use [Religion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=13) to add an [occult](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=3) spell to your bardic spell repertoire.

You must pay the materials for the spell listed in the table below and attempt the skill check.

**Critical Success** You expend half the materials and learn the spell.  
**Success** You expend the materials and learn the spell.  
**Failure** You fail to learn the spell but can try again after you gain a level. The materials aren't expended.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, except you expend half the materials.

| Spell Level    | Price    | Typical DC[^1] |
| -------------- | -------- | -------------- |
| 1st or cantrip | 2 gp     | 15             |
| 2nd            | 6 gp     | 18             |
| 3rd            | 16 gp    | 20             |
| 4th            | 36 gp    | 23             |
| 5th            | 70 gp    | 26             |
| 6th            | 140 gp   | 28             |
| 7th            | 300 gp   | 31             |
| 8th            | 650 gp   | 34             |
| 9th            | 1,500 gp | 36             |
| 10th           | 7,000 gp | 41             |

[^1]: Typically: +2 for uncommon, +5 for rare.
