## Campaign Etiquette & Rules

> [!abstract]
> Being on time, putting effort into session reports, and other campaign resources is encouraged and will be rewarded!

### Rules of Play

- **Devices**: No big laptops on the table. Be mindful of the space your phone, etc. takes up.
- **Art**: Credit the artist of your character's art (including AI art).
- **Etiquette**: Respect and common sense are key. Sessions in cafes require you to buy at least one drink.
- **Participation**: Sign up for sessions via the Discord `#🤝lfg` channel.
- **Cheating**: Don't do it. We log more than you think, and you shouldn't feel the need to cheat. If you feel left behind or particularly weak, contact a Voidmaster and we'll see what we can do for you.

### General Mechanics (D&D 2024)

- **Exhaustion**: Rules from 2024 PHB.
- **Flanking**: Provides a +2 bonus to hit.
- **Potions**:
  - Drinking a potion yourself is a **Bonus Action**.
  - Using an **Action** to drink a Healing Potion grants the **maximum** possible effect.
  - Potion carry limit = Proficiency Bonus.
- **Armor Class**: Matching an opponent's AC deals **half damage**.
- **Homebrew**: Items found in sessions are bound to the world they were found in.

### Rewards

- **Wiki Session Reports**: See [[_session_reports|Session Report Guidelines]].
- **Punctuality**: Being on time rewards you with **Half Level in gold pieces** (gp) in Pathfinder, and an extra Hero Point in D&D.
