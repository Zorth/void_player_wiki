> [!abstract]
> Sharing your adventures is encouraged and rewarded! There are two ways to share your reports, depending on the format and where you want them to live.

## Session Report Formats

### Wiki Reports (Objective)
The wiki is a resource for facts and lore. Session reports here should be:
- **Objective**: Focused on facts, events, and discoveries. What the Players know.
- **Information-Dense**: Use bullet points to list key events, NPCs met, and items found.
- **Template**: Use the [[_SESSIONNOTE]] template.
- **Location**: Save in the `/Session Reports` folder.

### Discord Reports (In-Character)
If you want to write a long-form, story-like, or in-character narrative, post it in the **[#session_reports](https://discord.com/channels/878674783972261918/1322276625928425482)** channel on Discord.

---

## Rewards

Session reports give you an extra **Hero Point** in the next session in that world!
