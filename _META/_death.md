> [!abstract]
> In The Void, death is not always the end. Powerful beings and rituals can bring fallen adventurers back from the beyond, for a price.

## Pathfinder 2e

- **Ritual**: The **Resurrect** and **Reincarnate** rituals are common knowledge for characters of **Level 5** and higher.
- **Location**: Resurrections must be performed exclusively within **The Void**.

## Dungeons & Dragons

In D&D, resurrection involves a revival fee based on the difficulty of the task and the character's history.

### Revival Fee Formula

$$
Fee = \text{Material Cost} + (Level \times 100 \text{ gp}) + (50 \text{ gp} \times 2^{\text{deaths}})
$$

**Example (First Death at Level 8):**
- Raise Dead (500 gp) + Level 8 (800 gp) + First death (100 gp) = **1400 gp**

---

### ⚠️ Equipment Loss

If a character's body is **not recovered** during the session, they lose **all of their equipment**!
