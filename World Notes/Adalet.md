#organization [[Kalogeron]]

**House Adalet** is a member house of the [[Divine Council]], known for its expertise in building and law. Its leader, [[Adoras Adalet]], is the Council's judge and embodies the [[Old Pantheon#Deities|Gilded Fist]].

### Neutrality
House Adalet is renowned for its absolute neutrality, so much so that all of [[Kalogeron]] trusts them to lead or overrule in cases of unresolved conflict between other houses.

### Vassals
- House [[Maimond]] (Architects & Construction)
- House [[Varithal]] (Lawyers)
