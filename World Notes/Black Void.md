#location [[The Void]]

> [!Quote]
> The Black Void is an elusive haven within the greater void realm, serving as a refuge for those who face trials beyond their control. Its boundaries are marked by dark corridors and hidden dangers, often used as an escape from conflicts between adventurers. Here, those with power or resources can retreat to privacy, facing challenges that test their resolve or abilities, whether escaping foes or leveraging their strength for others' downfall.
