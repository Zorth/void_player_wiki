#world

![[sythian_logo.svg]]

Sythian is the world ran by Jasper.

The world is divided into two major continents, **[[Wethrun]]** and **[[Elaor]]**, which are connected by a land bridge where the city-state of **[[Kalogeron]]** is situated.
