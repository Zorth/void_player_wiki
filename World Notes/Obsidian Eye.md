#organization [[Zenith]]

> [!abstract]
> An organization seeking access to vaults in [[Zenith]].

The Obsidian Eye is an organization that was actively seeking access to a vault located in a subterranean tomb north of [[Sharan]]. This vault was discovered to contain a portal to a shadow or fog dimension, possibly the [[Foglands]].
