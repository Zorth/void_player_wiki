#npc [[Kalogeron]]

> [!Quote]
> The House of Adalet is celebrated for its masterful lawyers and builders, guided by Adoras, who exemplifies the will of the Gilded Fist. As the council's judge and a loyal supporter of its leader, he stands as a symbol of justice and unwavering authority.
> Outside his official duties, he is a passionate architect who enjoys designing miniature models of projects for the city or his own residence.

He is great friends with [[Patriarch Vareren]], the leader of House [[Varithal]], continuing a long-standing tradition of friendship between the leaders of their respective houses.
