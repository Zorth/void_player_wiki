#event [[Kalogeron]]

> [!abstract]
> A historical conflict that led to the creation of [[Kalogeron]].

The **Tyrannical War** is a significant historical event in the history of [[Kalogeron]]. House [[Canción]] is known for performing a play and opera detailing the events of this war and the subsequent founding of the city.
