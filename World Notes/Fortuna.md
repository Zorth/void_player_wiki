#organization [[Kalogeron]]

> [!abstract]
> One of the ruling houses of [[Kalogeron]].

**House Fortuna** is led by [[Lady Fortuna]]. The house has strong cultural ties and maintains good relations with House [[Canción]]. Lady Fortuna is married to [[Lazar Ebrius]], the leader of House [[Ebrius]].
