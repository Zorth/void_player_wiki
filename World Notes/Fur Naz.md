#npc [[Kalogeron]]

The head of House [[Naz]], also known as **Scribe Fur**. He is known for having 12 simulacra; those seeking the original should ask for "**Naz Prime**".

Scribe Fur is the primary contact for those arriving in [[Kalogeron]] and works closely with the [[Divine Council]] to recruit adventurers.
