#organization [[Kalogeron]]

**House Prabuddh** is a member of the [[Divine Council]], known for its focus on self-improvement of both body and mind. Its patron, [[Iro Sou Prabuddh]], reflects the disciplined nature of the [[Old Pantheon#Deities|Master of Masters]].

### Additional Information
- Information on the house within the archives is specifically being safekept by monks from the [[Heavenly District]].
