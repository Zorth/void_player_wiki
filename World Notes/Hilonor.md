#world 

Hilonor is the world ran by Miel.

> [!quote]
> A new portal opened in the [[Well of Worlds]]. Its destination is Hilonor. A world that's behind in some discoveries in terms of magic and the likes while being leaps ahead in certain others.

## Geography

Hilonor is divided into two primary continents:
- [[Old Continent]]: Home to the [[Evolution Epicentre]].
- [[New Continent]]: Mentioned as the location of the [[Iron Fang Convoy]] leadership.

## Organizations & Customs

The most prominent organization for adventurers is the [[Iron Fang Convoy]].
