> [!Quote]
> The Well of Worlds of The Void is the town's square. Here the hustle and bustle of the city takes place. Lined around the edge of the large circle are portals to different worlds and universes. Here adventurers ready themselves to venture into distant lands and deep into dungeons. It's a dark and gothic town square with pillars and ornate details.
