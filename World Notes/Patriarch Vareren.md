#npc [[Kalogeron]]

> [!abstract]
> The leader of House [[Varithal]].

**Patriarch Vareren** is the current head of House [[Varithal]]. He is a renowned lawyer and is known for his long-standing friendship with [[Adoras Adalet]], the leader of House [[Adalet]]. This friendship follows a multi-generational tradition between the leaders of both houses.
