#location/settlement [[Sythian]]

Wamerwick is a town located East in the [[Grand Duchy of Hatham]]. The town lies South-West of [[Little Hold]] and South-East of [[Rothfield]].

As the town was bluid near the sea, a river runs around and trough it, giving the people some further fortifications long side the walls that they have build.
In the town itself you can find a variation of building, kind hearted people and lovey baked goods.
