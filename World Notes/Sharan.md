#location/settlement [[Zenith]]

Capital of the [[Sassanid|Sassanid Empire]]. A marvel of magical engineering and order, surrounded by double walls and divided into harmonized districts.

It hosts institutions such as the [[University of Nine Stars]].

> [!QUOTE|]
> The Spire Codes of Sharan
> By decree of the Crystal Spire and the Sultan’s Spear
Let all citizens and visitors to the City of Sharan heed the following ordinances:
> 
> I. Magic in the Streets
The use of magic is permitted in public, but citizens are reminded:
Spells that significantly alter people, property, or the environment may trigger automatic response by city regulators.
> 
> II. The Crystal Silence
Within sight of the Crystal Spire, spellcasting beyond the third tier is forbidden without sanctioned invitation.
This is for your safety and the harmony of the capital.
> 
> III. The River’s Sanctity
> The River Sharan and its aqueducts are sacred infrastructure.
> Any tampering—mundane or magical—will be met with full imperial justice.
> 
> IV. The Artisan’s Oath
> All enchanted goods produced in the Glassworks must bear registered sigils of origin and integrity.
> Unmarked enchantments will be seized and investigated.
> 
> V. Responding to Imperial Agents
> Should you be approached by a city official or enforcement triad, cease all movement and comply with instruction.
> Do not be alarmed. The peace of the Empire watches over you always.
> 
> Harmony flows from trust. Knowledge flows from harmony. Let both flourish in Sharan.
