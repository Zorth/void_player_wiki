Kalogeron is the metropolis city-state ran by Mattis. It is located on a land bridge connecting the continents of **[Wethrun](/Wethrun)** and **[Elaor](/Elaor)** on the world of **[Sythian](/Sythian)**.

# Kalogeron, Metropolis of the Divine

> [!Quote] [Scribe Fur](/Fur Naz), House [Naz](/Naz)
> Kalogeron, Metropolis of the Devine, the oldest city to exist on this world. A city bustling with people of all kinds, trade, beliefs and trouble.
>
> The Devine Council has heard of your potential and is extending it's hand for a partnership.
>
> You get rid of brewing trouble and as a reward you get paid handsomely.
>
> I will know when you arrive

Kalogeron is the oldest city to exist on Sythian, a bustling hub of trade, diverse beliefs, and persistent trouble. Its background allows for diverse character origins, often involving a recruiter from [The Void](/The Void) acceptance test.

## [Deities](/Old Pantheon)

The city's culture is deeply tied to the [Old Pantheon](/Old Pantheon), with many citizens choosing a patron deity to guide their character's path.

## The Great Houses of Kalogeron

### [Divine Council](/Divine Council)

The Divine Council is the governing body of the city, comprised of leaders who embody the virtues of their respective deities.

- [Lu'an Is](/Lu'an Is) (Matron: [Charis Lu'an Is](/Charis Lu'an Is))
- [Canción](/Canción) (Matron: [Syriss Canción](/Syriss Canción))
- [Prabuddh](/Prabuddh) (Patron: [Iro Sou Prabuddh](/Iro Sou Prabuddh))
- [Adalet](/Adalet) (Leader: [Adoras Adalet](/Adoras Adalet))
