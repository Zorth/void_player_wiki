#pc/apprentice 

ORIGIN: [[Zenith]]

> [!abstract]
> A dragonblood elf and thaumaturge of the [[Mystic Order]]. Once a high-ranking emissary to [[Crownspire|the capital]] of Muris, she now roams the Void as a shield-bearing wanderer, seeking her lost twin and a sense of belonging beyond her duty.

Raised in the mountain keep of the [[Mystic Order]], Lhothrash underwent the Trial of the Scales as a child—a ritual involving draconic blood transfusion that granted her the mutations necessary to combat esoteric threats. Following the trial, she was separated from her twin brother and trained as a thaumaturge, eventually serving as a long-term emissary to [[Crownspire]].

Her life of service was upended when [[Saxon Parisi|the King of Muris]] was assassinated. Suspected as an outsider, Lhothrash fled [[Crownspire|the capital]], losing her armor and nearly her life in the process. After a brush with death against an evil spirit, she was saved by a magical butterfly, setting her on her current path.

Though she continues to provide thaumaturgical services in the name of her Order, she secretly searches for traces of her lost brother. Beyond her mechanical devotion to her work, she craves to be valued as a person rather than just a tool of the Order.
